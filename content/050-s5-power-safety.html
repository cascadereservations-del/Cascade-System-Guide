Power and safety when you leave

At the end of a turnover the app shows Power & Safety Instructions. Which one to follow depends on when the next guest arrives.

The "everything OFF" line does not say what to do with the fridge. If you are not sure, ask Lloyd or Marifel before you leave. Do not guess.

The app also asks for two final checks in the power section:

  • EcoFlow RIVER 3 stowed in its spot, cable tidy. Check it is there and not damaged. If it is missing, report it at once.
  • Final power & safety check. This is marked Critical. Every window is latched, the door is locked and all lights are off.

The Confirm & Leave list also asks for the EcoFlow at 100% and the air conditioner at 24°C. See Confirm & Leave.

Appliances in short

The full guides are in the Manual, under Training & Guides on the Dashboard (see Appliance guides and videos). Here is the short version.

  • When power returns, plug the EcoFlow into the wall. A full charge takes about 1 to 2 hours.
  • At every turnover the EcoFlow must be charged to 100%. The app asks you to confirm it. If it reads under 100%, leave it charging and do not close your session until it is full.

Air conditioner remote

  • Guests are comfortable at 26°C, mode COOL, fan AUTO.
  • The Mode button cycles COOL, FAN, DRY and AUTO. SLEEP raises the temperature 1°C each hour. ECO saves power. SWING moves the louver. TIMER switches it off after some hours.
  • Leaving after a cleaning: set the air conditioner to 24°C. The Cleaner app asks you to confirm it in Confirm & Leave.
  • Turn it fully off if the unit will be empty for 24 hours or more.

Water heater

  • A red light means it is heating. Light off or green means the water is ready.
  • Switch it ON before a shower. Wait 3 to 5 minutes for hot water.
  • Turn it OFF after every guest checks out. Turn it ON 10 to 15 minutes before the next guest arrives.
  • Never switch it on if the water supply is off. It will burn out.

Induction cooker

  • Use pans with a magnetic base or the coil symbol. Aluminum, copper, glass and ceramic do not heat.
  • Place the pan in the centre, press POWER, then use + and − to set the level. Levels 4 to 5 are normal cooking.
  • It switches itself off if no pan is detected for 60 seconds.
  • Let the glass cool, then wipe with a damp cloth. Never put it in water and never use rough pads.

Air purifier

  • At each arrival, check that it is on and the filter light is green.
  • In the weekly deep tasks, clean the dust from the front grille with a brush and wipe the outside. Do not remove or wash the inside filter.

Non-stick pans

Use gentle methods on coated pans. Avoid rough pads and hard scrubbing, which scratch the coating. The Manual has short videos on removing burnt marks safely.

Brownout days

A brownout is a planned power cut. When one is coming, the OPS group gets a notice that says the power will be off, and when. The notice comes in the OPS group and also in the morning summary.

  1. The day before: charge the EcoFlow to full.
  2. Keep the emergency light ready in the unit.
  3. During the cut: use the EcoFlow only for the things in the "You can power" list.
  4. When power returns: plug the EcoFlow in again and check the fridge is cold.

Guests are told about a brownout only if they ask. Cuts that nobody announced are not in the notice, so you may not get a warning. See the tree below.

The 5-star standard, in short

Cascade promises Hotel Comfort. Home Warmth. Every action should feel warm, calm and confident.

  • No bad smell when a guest walks in.
  • The bed is made hotel-style, with tight corners and fluffed pillows.
  • Surfaces, mirrors and glass are clean and streak-free.
  • The basics are restocked, so a guest never has to ask for toilet paper or coffee.
  • Everything works: lights, AC, fridge, lock, WiFi.
