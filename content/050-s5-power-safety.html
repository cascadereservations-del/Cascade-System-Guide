Power and safety when you leave

At the end of a turnover the app shows Power & Safety Instructions. Which one to follow depends on when the next guest arrives.

The "everything OFF" line does not say what to do with the fridge. If you are not sure, ask Lloyd or Marifel before you leave. Do not guess.

The app also asks for two final checks in the power section:

  • EcoFlow RIVER 3 stowed in its spot, cable tidy. Check it is there and not damaged. If it is missing, report it at once.
  • Final power & safety check. This is marked Critical. Every window is latched, the door is locked and all lights are off.

The Confirm & Leave list also asks for the EcoFlow at 100% and the air conditioner at 24°C. See Confirm & Leave.

Appliances in short

The full guides are in the Manual, under Training & Guides on the Dashboard (see Appliance guides and videos). Here is the short version.

EcoFlow (the backup battery)

The EcoFlow is permanent equipment inside the unit. It is not borrowable. It never leaves Cascade. If it is missing, tell Lloyd at once.

  • When power returns, plug the EcoFlow into the wall. A full charge takes about 1 to 2 hours.
  • At every turnover the EcoFlow must be charged to 100%. The app asks you to confirm it. If it reads under 100%, leave it charging and do not close your session until it is full.

Air conditioner remote

  • The aircon remote and the TV remote are kept together on the wooden tray on top of the TV cabinet, just below the TV, beside the flower vase.
  • Guests are comfortable at 26°C, mode COOL, fan AUTO.
  • The Mode button cycles COOL, FAN, DRY and AUTO. SLEEP raises the temperature 1°C each hour. ECO saves power. SWING moves the louver. TIMER switches it off after some hours.
  • Leaving after a cleaning: set the air conditioner to 24°C. The Cleaner app asks you to confirm it in Confirm & Leave.
  • Turn it fully off if the unit will be empty for 24 hours or more.

Water heater

  • A red light means it is heating. Light off or green means the water is ready.
  • Switch it ON before a shower. Wait 3 to 5 minutes for hot water.
  • Turn it OFF after every guest checks out. Turn it ON 10 to 15 minutes before the next guest arrives.
  • Never switch it on if the water supply is off. It will burn out.

Induction cooker

  • Use pans with a magnetic base or the coil symbol. Aluminum, copper, glass and ceramic do not heat.
  • Place the pan in the centre, press POWER, then use + and − to set the level. Levels 4 to 5 are normal cooking.
  • It switches itself off if no pan is detected for 60 seconds.
  • Let the glass cool, then wipe with a damp cloth. Never put it in water and never use rough pads.

Air purifier

  • At each arrival, check that it is on and the filter light is green.
  • In the weekly deep tasks, clean the dust from the front grille with a brush and wipe the outside. Do not remove or wash the inside filter.

Non-stick pans

Use gentle methods on coated pans. Avoid rough pads and hard scrubbing, which scratch the coating. The Manual has short videos on removing burnt marks safely.

Brownout days

A brownout is a planned power cut. When one is coming, the OPS group gets a notice that says the power will be off, and when. The notice comes in the OPS group and also in the morning summary.

  1. The day before: charge the EcoFlow to full.
  2. Keep the emergency light ready in the unit.
  3. During the cut: use the EcoFlow only for the things in the "You can power" list.
  4. When power returns: plug the EcoFlow in again and check the fridge is cold.

Guests are told about a brownout only if they ask. Cuts that nobody announced are not in the notice, so you may not get a warning. See the tree below.

The Cascade standard look

This is what a finished, guest-ready unit looks like. After you clean, compare each area with its photo.

Bed

Check these 3 things

  1. Make the bed hotel-style. Pull the sheets tight, with no bumps or folds, and tuck in all sides.
  2. Plump every pillow and set them in a straight, neat row.
  3. No stains on any sheet or pillowcase. You checked each one when you stripped the bed.

Room

Check these 3 things

  1. Dust everything from the top down: fan blades, shelves, bedside tables and skirting boards.
  2. High-touch surfaces are disinfected: light switches, door handles, remote controls and bedside lamps.
  3. Room items are straight and the mirrors have zero streaks.

Living area

Check these 3 things

  1. The floor is vacuumed first, then mopped. Work backward toward the door.
  2. No foul smell. Check the sofa too, even when the unit has been closed up.
  3. Walk through as the guest. Look for fingerprints, dust and smudges from different angles.

TV wall

Check these 3 things

  1. The TV unit is dusted, with nothing left on it that does not belong.
  2. The mirror is polished with a dry microfibre cloth. No streaks.
  3. Water the plant only if the soil is completely dry all the way down. Push a finger 2 cm into the soil. If you are not sure, skip it.

Remote tray

Check these 3 things

  1. Both remotes (aircon and TV) are on the wooden tray, and the tray is beside the vase. The remotes are wiped clean.
  2. The key card is on its Cascade Hideaway keychain, on the tray with the remotes. The gate pod lock is kept indoors with the key card and remotes.
  3. The aircon is set to 24°C before you leave.

Kitchen

Check these 3 things

  1. The dishes are washed and the stove is degreased.
  2. The utensil set is complete, counted against the list in the Cleaner app. Flag anything missing, chipped or broken.
  3. Plates, glasses and cookware have no chips or cracks. Flag any damage and note how many.

Kitchen shelf

Check these 3 things

  1. The microwave (inside and door), the kettle and the rice cooker are clean inside and outside.
  2. The basics are restocked, such as coffee. A guest should never have to ask for them.
  3. The mugs are complete and have no chips: 4 white mugs and 2 coloured mugs.

Sink

Check these 3 things

  1. The sink basin is scrubbed, the faucet is polished to a shine, and the countertops are wiped clean and dry.
  2. The dishwashing sponge is a fresh one. You threw away the used one.
  3. Soap and dish soap are refilled to the standard level.

Bathroom

Check these 3 things

  1. The toilet, the shower and the sink are scrubbed, rinsed and dry.
  2. Smell-check the toilet. Flush more than once. The vent grille is wiped.
  3. Shampoo, soap and toilet paper are refilled to the standard level.

Laundry corner

Check these 3 things

  1. The washing machine is reset: the drum is empty and the door is left open.
  2. The cloth steamer is reset: the tank is empty and the cord is coiled.
  3. Count the wardrobe hangers. You need at least 8. If there are fewer, flag it in the report.

Entrance

Check these 3 things

  1. Final power and safety check: every window is latched, the door is locked and all lights are off.
  2. The key card opens the door, and the guest code opens the lock cleanly.
  3. The “Waiting for Check In” automation is ON in SmartLife. Do this last, after you lock up.

Towels

There is no separate photo for towels. The bathroom photo above shows how they look.

Check these 3 things

  1. Every towel is fresh and has no stains. You inspected each one and pre-treated stains before laundry.
  2. You bagged all used towels and sheets in Phase 1, so no used linen is left out.
  3. The towels are folded neatly, as in the bathroom photo above.

The 5-star standard, in short

Cascade promises Hotel Comfort. Home Warmth. Every action should feel warm, calm and confident.

  • No bad smell when a guest walks in.
  • The bed is made hotel-style, with tight corners and fluffed pillows.
  • Surfaces, mirrors and glass are clean and streak-free.
  • The basics are restocked, so a guest never has to ask for toilet paper or coffee.
  • Everything works: lights, AC, fridge, lock, WiFi.
