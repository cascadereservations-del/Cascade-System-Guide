The admin dashboard is the office screen. It shows who is in the house, who is coming, what needs attention, and lets you decide bookings, log stock and check the money. This chapter walks through each page and each button.

How to read the badges

Each button below carries a label so you know what you are about to do.

Some old lines on screen still say "two-factor", and the account menu may show a small "No 2FA" label. Ignore all of it. There is no second step today. Your name and PIN are enough.

Signing in

  1. Open the dashboard on your phone or computer. The owner will give you the address.
  2. Type your Your name and your 4-digit PIN.
  3. Tap Sign in.
  4. The owner and Marifel land on Today. A cleaner lands on Operations.

If you see That name and PIN did not match. Check both and try again., check your spelling and the four digits. If it still fails, ask the owner to reset your PIN. If you see This account has no active staff access, your login was switched off. Ask the owner.

The link E-mail me a sign-in code only works for a real e-mail address. Staff logins do not have one, so do not use it.

To sign out, open the Account menu (your initial, top right) and tap Sign out.

The top bar

  • Toggle navigation opens or closes the menu. Shortcut: Ctrl+B (Cmd+B on a Mac).
  • Search or jump to… opens the global search. See the next section.
  • Apps (grid icon) has the same links as the Apps group.
  • The sun or moon icon lets you pick Light, Dark or System. It is saved on this device only.
  • The Account menu shows your name and role, and has Sign out.

Messages appear at the bottom right. Green means saved. Red means it did not work. Read the words in red. They tell you why.

Global search

Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac), or tap Search or jump to…. Type at least two letters.

  • Records finds bookings by guest name or code, guests by name, phone or e-mail (owner and Admin only), stock items, cleaning reports and follow-up tasks. Tap a result to open it.
  • Pages lists every page you may open. Type cal and choose Bookings › Calendar.

Search only reads. It never changes anything. If it says No matches, try fewer letters.

Undo toasts and the change history

After many saves, the green message has an Undo button for 8 seconds. Tap it and the record goes back to how it was. The undo is also written in the history.

Has an UndoHas no Undo
Follow-ups, work orders, notices, manual transactions and voids, shopping-list changes, meter flags, stock adjustments Booking decisions and cancellations, journals (use Reverse), stock counts, price changes, staff changes, guest merges, companions, cleaning evidence and readiness reviews

Every change that can be undone is also listed in Settings > Audit history. The owner and Marifel can undo it from there.

How lists behave

  • Your filters are saved in the address. The Back button and a copied link bring the same view back. Clear filters appears when a filter is on.
  • Tap a column heading to sort. It sorts only the 25 rows on the page you are looking at. Use Previous page and Next page to move.
  • Columns shows or hides columns. Some lists have a Comfortable or Compact switch.
  • A copy icon next to a code, phone or e-mail copies it. It turns into a tick.
  • A dash (—) means "not known". It never means zero.
  • A red box with Retry means the page could not load. It does not mean everything is fine. Tap Retry.
  • Save forms protect you from a double tap after a bad signal. Still, press a save button once and wait for the message.
  • Money shows as PHP. All times are Manila time.

Today

Owner Admin Cleaner Your morning page. Nothing can be changed here. Every card is a link to the record behind it. It refreshes by itself every 2 minutes.

At the bottom you see Source updated and Calendar sync times. That is how old the data is.

Bookings list

Owner Admin Cleaner One list of Airbnb stays, direct bookings and calendar blocks. You can only read it here. Tap a row to open the booking.

  • Unresolved payments shows direct bookings with no receipt or deposit recorded.
  • The last column shows the calendar link: Linked, Date match or No event.
  • The footer says Calendar last synced.

Calendar

Owner Admin Cleaner A month grid of who stays which nights. Read only. Dragging does nothing on purpose.

A stay covers the check-in night but not the check-out night. Cancelled stays are not drawn.

Inquiries

Owner Admin to decide. Everyone can read. Direct booking requests (each one is an inquiry) from the booking site wait here for a yes or no.

ButtonWhat happensBadge

Decline then Decline in the windowThe request is closed. The guest is not told by the system.Cannot be undone

The dashboard stops you if the payment has no approved review yet: No approved Finance review of this booking's payment yet. Approve the receipt first in the Finance review queue. If two people decide the same booking, the second sees Those dates are no longer available or this decision already exists. That is safe.

The full path from request to confirmation is in A booking from start to finish.

Booking detail

Owner Admin Cleaner Tap any booking to open it. Only Cancel booking can change anything, and only for direct bookings.

Cards on the page: States, Stay, Charges, Payment evidence and ledger, Cleaning, Timeline and Conversations. Buttons: Back and Guest profile.

Cancel booking

  1. Open the direct booking. Tap Cancel booking.
  2. In Cancel this booking, type the Reason (required). Three letters or more.
  3. Tap Cancel booking to confirm, or Keep booking to stop.
  4. Read the message Booking cancelled.

Cannot be undone The guest is told automatically. The calendar dates are freed. No refund is made. Money is not touched. A refund has to be handled by the owner or Marifel outside this button.

There is no refund button and no change-dates button in the dashboard today. Some text on the Guests page mentions refunds on Bookings. That text is out of date.

Operations: Work orders

Owner Admin create and change. Everyone can read. A work order is a maintenance job. One marked Blocks the next arrival holds the unit from being ready.

  1. Tap New work order.
  2. Fill Title (3 letters or more), Description, Priority, Status and Due.
  3. Tick Blocks the next arrival if the next guest cannot stay until it is fixed.
  4. Tap Save. Can undo

When you set the status to resolved, the Resolution (required) box appears. To remove one, open it, use the red Delete (cancel) this work order box with a reason. The list shows only open jobs unless you change Status. Urgent cleaner notes also arrive here as work orders. See Telegram: the two groups.

Operations: Notices

Owner Admin create and change. A notice is a short warning with a type (power, water, maintenance, weather, general), an audience and an end time. Active ones show on Today.

  1. Tap New notice.
  2. Fill Title, Description, Type, Audience, Effective date and Expires.
  3. Keep Active ticked. Tap Save. Can undo

A notice turns expired when its time passes or you untick Active. To remove one, use Delete (deactivate) this notice with a reason.

Inventory: Stock

Owner Admin Cleaner to look. Only the owner and Admin can receive or adjust stock. Shows what is on hand and what is running low.

Receive stock

  1. Tap the item to open its sheet.
  2. In Receive stock fill Packs, Cost per pack (PHP), Supplier and Date.
  3. Tap Record receipt once.
  4. Read Received and the number. Note: the expense is not posted to Finance. Record the expense there separately.

Adjust a wrong quantity

  1. Open the item. In Adjust (movement ledger only) type the Change (+/−) and a Reason.
  2. Tap Record adjustment.
  3. To take it back, tap Undo in the message within 8 seconds.

If the button says Needs a baseline count first, do a count on the Counts page first. An empty movement history is not zero stock.

Inventory: Counts

Owner Admin A count sheet. Each row has Recorded, Counted, a live Variance, a Note (where you counted, 3 letters or more) and Confirm. Nothing is saved until you press Confirm on that row.

Confirm sets the stock to your number. There is no Undo toast. Count carefully first. If you get it wrong, fix it with an Adjust.

Settings: System health

Owner Admin Cleaner Everyone can open this page. It reloads every minute and reports missing records as missing, not as healthy.

Sections: Verification checks, System verifier findings, Client errors, Job heartbeats, Calendar sync, Airbnb e-mail ingestion and Data integrity report. An empty findings list says Nothing open. The verifier runs every hour and again at 07:45.

Run all checks only reads and compares. Known, stop reminding stops the reminder for one finding until something about it changes.

What a cleaner account sees

The cleaner view looks like the owner screens above, with a shorter menu and no money columns.

  • Can open: Today, Bookings, Operations and Inventory.
  • Cannot open: Guests, Finance and the other office pages.
  • Cannot do: create work orders or notices, decide inquiries, receive or adjust stock, review cleaning evidence or readiness.
  • Money columns are hidden. But a cleaner can still read guest names and cleaning reports.

A cleaner who taps Settings sees the grey card Not available for your role. That page is Staff. The one page a cleaner may use is System health. Open Settings, then choose System health.
