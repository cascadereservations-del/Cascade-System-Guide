Cascade has two sides. The guest side is what guests see. The staff side is what the team uses to keep the stay running. This page shows how they connect.

The map

Read the picture from left to right. A guest finds us, books, stays and leaves. Each step has a staff task behind it. Tap a box in the picture to jump to the chapter about it.

The guest side

  • Booking site. The guest picks dates, sees the price and sends a booking request with a payment receipt.
  • Messenger. The guest can ask questions or book here. Cassy answers first and calls the host when a person is needed.
  • Guest guide. After booking, the guest opens it for check-in steps, house help and things to do nearby.
  • Airbnb. Some guests book on Airbnb. Those bookings reach our system by e-mail and calendar sync.

The staff side

  • Operations Manual. The standards and how-to guides for the house.
  • Cleaner app. Where the cleaner records every clean with photos, meter readings and a checklist.
  • Telegram. Two groups, Finance and OPS. Cards arrive here when something needs a person.
  • Admin dashboard. The office view of bookings, guests, cleaning reports, stock and money.

Where information lives

The dashboard shows the system of record: the official place for bookings, guests, cleaning reports, stock and money. Telegram cards, e-mails and the Manual are ways to reach that record. They are not the record itself.

If a Telegram card and the dashboard disagree, the dashboard is right. Tell Lloyd what you saw.
