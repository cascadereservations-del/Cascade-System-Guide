Set up your phone

Do this once. It takes about ten minutes.

Open the link in your phone browser. Use Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone. The links are in the table above.

Open the browser menu. Choose Add to Home Screen or Install app. The app now has its own icon, like any other app.

The Cleaner app needs the camera for photos. When your phone asks, choose Allow. If you said no by mistake, open your phone settings, find your browser and turn the camera on. Then reload the app.

Do not clear your browser history, cookies or site data. The Cleaner app keeps your draft in the browser. If you clear it, the draft is lost. A draft is kept for about 24 hours.

Enter your own PIN or sign-in name. Type it yourself, with nobody watching. If it does not work, ask Lloyd.

Install Telegram and ask Lloyd to add you to the OPS group. Then type /menu in the group. A menu of buttons appears.

The Cascade Staff app

This small app shows what is happening at the house today: who is staying, which nights are free, and what needs a person. It also has buttons that open Telegram. Add it to your home screen like the other apps.

Guest Calendar Info

Open Guest Calendar Info from Home, or tap the Calendar tab. Each day in the month is one of three kinds. The kinds differ by pattern as well as colour, so you can tell them apart in bright sun.

Day looks likeIt meansWhat to do
Free - an empty, outlined dayNobody is staying that night and nothing holds it.Nothing. The night can be booked.
Blocked - a hatched day with a small iconThe night is held, so it cannot be booked. The icon shows why.Tap the day to read the reason, such as a direct booking, an owner hold or maintenance.
Booked - a filled day, with the guest's first letter on the first nightA guest stays that night.Tap the day, then Show the stay to see the dates.

A small icon at the top right of a day is a day marker. It says something else is going on that day, on top of free, blocked or booked. The markers are:

  • Brownout - a planned power cut. It is usually added by the system.
  • Maintenance - a repair or a visit by a technician.
  • Deep cleaning - a longer clean than a normal turnover.
  • Note - any other short note for the day.

Tap any day to open its details. The sheet names the status, the reason for a blocked night, and every marker on that day. Tap Close when you are done.

Under the month you also see In the house, Next, Coming up and Blocked nights. Blocked nights lists each held night with its reason.

Warnings

The Warnings list at the bottom shows brownouts, low stock and system checks. Every line is a button. Tap a warning and a sheet opens with three things:

  1. What the warning means, in plain words.
  2. The facts: the item, the dates, how many.
  3. A Go to button, when there is a place to fix it. Tap it to open that page.

If a warning has no Go to button, it is for the owner or an admin to handle. Tell Lloyd or Marifel in OPS and carry on with your work.

Telegram buttons

Home has a button for the OPS group. The Finance button is for people who use Finance. Tap one and it opens the Telegram app straight on that group. If the app does not open, the page tells you and a second tap opens Telegram in the browser instead.

What is not sent to your sign-in

Guest phone numbers and e-mail addresses are not sent to your sign-in. If you need to reach a guest, ask Lloyd or Marifel.

PIN and password rules

  1. Your PIN or password is yours only. Every person has their own.
  2. Never share it. Never lend your phone while you are signed in.
  3. Never write it in a chat, a message, a photo or a note. No one on the team will ask you for it.
  4. If you forget it, or think someone saw it, tell Lloyd right away. He will reset it.

This guide never shows a PIN, a door code or a Wi-Fi password. If someone asks you to type one into a chat, do not do it. Tell Lloyd.
