Your first week

This is a simple plan. Go at your own speed. Ask questions early.

  1. Day 1. Read Welcome, The big picture and Your apps and sign-ins. Set up your phone and sign in.
  2. Day 2. Read the Manual tour. Look through the Cleaning page in the Manual.
  3. Days 3 and 4. Walk through a turnover with Honey. Read the turnover chapter and power and safety beside her.
  4. Day 5. Do a turnover yourself with Honey close by. Ask about anything that was unclear.
  5. Day 2. Read the dashboard tour. Open each page and look, without changing anything.
  6. Days 3 and 4. Read A booking from start to finish and Guests and Messenger. Watch Marifel handle real cards.
  7. Day 5. Answer a guest with a Cassy draft while Marifel checks it before you send.
  8. Everyone, at the end of the week. Read Telegram, Cassy and When something goes wrong. Tick the boxes you can do. Talk with Lloyd about the ones you cannot.

It is fine to ask. A short question early is better than a mistake later.
