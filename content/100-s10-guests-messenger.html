Guests meet Cascade in three places: the booking site before they book, the guest guide during their stay, and Messenger any time. This chapter shows what they see and what you do when the Messenger concierge needs the host.

What a guest sees on the booking site

The booking site is where a guest finds the property, checks dates and requests a booking. The full path is in A booking from start to finish. It also shows photos, the house rules, check-in at 2:00 PM and check-out at 12:00 noon. The exact address stays hidden until a booking is confirmed.

Public link: the booking site.

The guest guide

The guest guide is a private page for people who booked. It holds the door instructions, the Wi-Fi, the house tips, maps and the check-out list. It opens behind a small gate.

Public link: the guest guide.

The gate

  1. The guest picks their Check-in Date.
  2. The guide checks it and asks: What's the first letter of the name on your booking?
  3. The guest types the first letter in Booking Name Initial and taps Open My Guide.

If a guest cannot get in, ask: what check-in date did you book, and whose name is on the booking? The name might be a friend's. If the date and the letter are right and it still fails, tell the owner.

On the day of check-in, the guide shows a priority card with the arrival details first.

The 24 hour Messenger rule

Facebook lets the page send a normal message to a guest only within 24 hours after the guest's last message. The system therefore treats a guest as reachable on Messenger for 23 hours. After that it sends its messages by e-mail instead. The host reply buttons use a special Facebook tag, so they can still reach a guest up to 7 days after the guest's last message.

When a guest is handed to the host

A handoff happens when the guest's message is about one of the topics below, or the concierge is unsure, or the chat has run long. A card arrives in the OPS group.

Things that surprise people:

  • After a handoff, the concierge keeps answering the guest's other questions. She does not go quiet. After a safety report she pauses for 24 hours, but a safety or lock-out message always gets through.
  • When Marifel or Lloyd types a reply straight in the Facebook Page inbox, Cassy goes quiet on that chat for 30 days from the latest reply. She sends the guest nothing more. The guest's open handoffs are closed, and the hosts see each new guest message as a notice in OPS. Only a person answers that guest now.
  • The same question inside 24 hours does not make a second card. A new message on the same matter arrives as Follow-up to #CH-SAMPLE, with no options.
  • Sometimes the concierge is set to suggest only. Then the guest is told the host will reply personally, and OPS gets a 💬 GUEST · draft card with a suggested reply and no buttons. If the message is not routine, the card adds a line that starts ⚠️ This reads as (a payment claim, refund, cancellation, complaint, safety or access issue) with the rule to follow: check Finance before believing a payment, never promise money back outside the 5-day rule, call the guest for safety. Write your own reply.
  • Two smaller host cards: 💬 GUEST · handoff · attachment means the guest sent a photo or file that is not a payment receipt - open the chat and look. 💬 GUEST · glance means the concierge answered but wants a person to check its answer (or it could not read the calendar and told the guest the dates will be confirmed) - read the chat and reply if needed.

How to answer a handoff

  1. Open the card in the OPS group. Read the guest's words at the top.
  2. Read Option 1 and Option 2. Choose the one that fits.
  3. Tap Send option 1 or Send option 2. Or tap ✍️ Write my own.
  4. For your own words, reply to the card that has the code #CH- at the bottom. Type your message. Send.
  5. Check the card changed to show your reply.

The OPS group never carries money. If the guest asks about a payment or a refund, look at the Finance group and the dashboard, and tell the guest the host will check.

You can also answer in the Facebook Page inbox. The card has a link to it. Past handoffs are listed on the Guests page, under Messenger handoffs.

A guest who cannot get in

An access, safety or priority handoff is urgent. It also posts a red note in the Finance group, and an e-mail titled URGENT goes to the host inbox, so someone who is awake sees it. These extra notes have no buttons. Answer from the OPS card.

  1. Answer the guest at once. Use Send option 1 or reply to the card.
  2. Set a new code in the lock app, or send the on-ground person. See The door code.
  3. If the guest is in danger, they must call the emergency number first.
  4. Tell the owner what happened.
