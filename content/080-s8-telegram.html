Cascade talks to its team in Telegram. One bot posts cards in two groups. A card tells you what happened and the one thing to do. This chapter shows every card and every button, so you know what a tap does before you tap it.

The two groups

The bot knows the two groups apart and ignores every other chat. Use the right group for the right job.

If you are unsure which group to use, use OPS for anything about the house, the guests or the cleaning. Anything about money goes in Finance.

Who may tap what

There are three levels. The bot checks the level when you tap.

  1. Anyone in the group. Expense confirm and cancel, receipt confirm, edit and discard, mark a cleaning fee paid, save a notice, menu buttons, Show as text, Revise, save a brownout. There is no identity check.
  2. Mapped staff only. Confirm booking and Decline, Apply on a stock count, Known, stop reminding, and the answers on a blocked-date card. Mapped staff means your Telegram account is linked to your staff profile.
  3. Finance group, or a mapped owner or admin. Save it on a house-fact card from Cassy.

If a button answers that your Telegram account is not mapped to a staff profile, nothing was changed. Ask Lloyd to map your account. The buttons stay on the card for someone who is mapped.

If you log or confirm something by mistake, tell Lloyd. Telegram has no Undo button for money.

Things that look like commands but are not

/weather, /calendar and /turnover are not commands. They are menu buttons only. Open /menu and tap the button. In OPS a typed one is ignored. In Finance it just reopens the menu.

A Finance-only command typed in the OPS group is silently ignored. You get no reply at all. This is normal. Go to the Finance group to run it. You only see "That one runs from the Finance group." if you tap the menu-button version.

Stock counts

  1. In Finance, type /count. Pick a group: Consumables, Stores or All groups.
  2. The bot posts a card with one button per item. Each button shows the system quantity.
  3. Tap an item. The bot asks for the new count. Type just the number, like 20. Type 0 if none is left.
  4. Changed items show as 3 - Item - 5 -> 2 on the card. You can also reply with lines like 2 20 and 7 0 to set many at once.
  5. Tap Apply to save. Only mapped staff can. Nothing is saved before that.

Apply is refused if the stock moved since you opened the list, or if the item is movement-controlled. Open a fresh /count and try again. Apply cannot be undone. To fix a wrong count, count again.

Every card, and what its buttons do

Below is a replica of every card the bot posts. The data is made up. Tap a button to read what really happens. Nothing is sent from this page.

The first line of a card starts with an emoji that tells you how serious it is:

  • 🟢 DAILY - a normal report.
  • 🟡 ATTENTION - something needs a look.
  • 🔴 ALERT - act now.
  • 🏠 NEW BOOKING, 💬 GUEST, 🧹 CLEANING, 🧾 FINANCE, 📋 WEEKLY - the topic.

Most cards end with a Do: line. That is the one thing to do. A card that says Do: send ... this carries the ready text on a line that starts with 📨.

These put money or data in place at once for whoever taps them: ✅ Received Payment (OPS), /void and /brownout. Read the card before you tap.
