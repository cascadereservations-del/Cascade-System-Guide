Signing in

  1. Open the Manual.
  2. Tap your PIN on the keypad.
  3. Wait. The screen opens on the Dashboard.

Your PIN is personal. Do not write it down, do not send it in a message, and do not tell anyone. This guide never shows a PIN.

Forgot your PIN? Ask Lloyd.

SOPs: the 5-star way to talk to guests

SOP means standard operating procedure. The SOPs page has the 5-star standard from the first message to the review after the stay. It has five parts:

  1. Inquiry & Booking: First Impressions
  2. Pre-Arrival: Build Anticipation
  3. Post-Stay: A Gracious Farewell
  4. Guest Screening: Protecting the Property
  5. Emergency & Complaint Response

Most guest messages are answered by the office. The SOPs still show you what a great stay feels like, and why small details matter.

The Cleaning page

The Cleaning page is the standard for every turnover. Read it once, then use it as a reminder. It has a green button, Open Cleaning Checklist App — Submit Report, that opens the Cleaner app.

Manual phaseTimeThe main points
Phase 1: Entry & Reset15 minutesOpen windows. Inspect for damage and photograph it before you clean. Strip and bag linens.
Phase 2: Deep Clean45 minutesBathroom: spray, wait 5 minutes, scrub, rinse, dry. Kitchenette: dishes, stove, sink, counters, microwave, fridge. Dust from top to bottom and sanitize remotes, switches and handles.
Phase 3: Staging & Final Touches30 minutesHotel-style bed with tight corners. Pillows fluffed. Restock the basics. Vacuum, then mop from the far corner to the door.

Odor control is very important. The unit must have no foul smell when a guest walks in. Check the sofa, even when it is closed up. If the last guest smoked, take extra care with linens and fabric. Use a fabric deodorizer and sanitize the whole unit after cleaning.

Safety. Wear gloves, especially with cleaning chemicals. Keep windows open for fresh air.

Microfiber tip. Use microfiber cloths for streak-free glass and faucets. Use a separate cloth for the toilet.

Send at least 5 after-clean photos before you submit. 12 to 14 is ideal. The Cleaner app will not let you submit with fewer.

Appliance guides and videos

On the Dashboard, under Training & Guides, five buttons open a small window with a quick guide. Read it, then close it with the ✕.

  • EcoFlow River: what it can and cannot power during a brownout.
  • Aircon Remote: the buttons and the right settings.
  • TV Remote: power, volume, input and streaming.
  • Water Heater: the indicator lights and when to switch it on and off.
  • Induction Cooker: which pans work and how to clean it.

Other buttons open guides for the Air Purifier, Dust Mite Vac and Iron. Non-stick pan care and the Air Purifier guide also have their own pages with videos. The full short version is in Power, safety and appliances.

The Videos page has short training videos. Watch one, then follow the 2 to 4 key steps written under it. It includes a spotless-cleaning checklist that works from dry to wet and from top to bottom.

The Playbook page has plans for a crisis, and the Maintenance page holds the maintenance log. Read the Playbook once so you know it is there.
