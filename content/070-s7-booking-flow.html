A direct booking has two halves. The guest asks for the dates and sends proof of payment. Then the office checks the money and confirms. After that the system sends the guest their messages by itself, except the door code, which you send.

Part 1: What the guest does on the booking site

The guest uses the public booking site. You can walk through the same screens on your own phone. Do not send a real request to test it.

At the top of the page the guest taps Check-in and Check-out and chooses dates on the small calendar. Then they tap Check dates. The page shows if the dates are free. The calendar is the live calendar, so taken nights cannot be picked.

The stay is 1 night or more. Longer stays get a discount that shows as the guest picks dates: 5% from 2 nights, 10% from 7 nights, 25% from 28 nights.

The guest fills in Full Name, Contact Number (Phone or WhatsApp), Email Address and Guests (1 to 3 adults). There is an optional Special Requests box for early check-in, a child, or an occasion. All the other boxes are required.

Then they tap Review price & payment.

The guest sees the price, then chooses how much to pay now:

  • Reserve (50%) holds the dates now. It is a reservation fee of half the total. It is offered only when check-in is 5 days away or more.
  • Pay in Full pays everything. When check-in is less than 5 days away, this is the only choice. There is no 50% reservation.

They then pick Bank Transfer or GCash. The page shows the payment details and buttons to copy the account number and the amount.

The guest sends the money in their own banking app, then taps Tap to upload under Attach Your Payment Screenshot. It accepts JPG, PNG or PDF up to 5 MB. Then they tap Submit reservation request. (After a late-booking notice, the button reads I've sent the payment.)

A box says Reservation request received, with a reference such as DIR-SAMPLE. It says the dates are not confirmed until a personal confirmation arrives. It also says the balance and the deposit are collected at least a day before check-in.

Part 2: What the office does

When the guest submits, two cards appear in the Finance group in Telegram. The first says a request came in and the dates are on hold. The second, a few moments later, holds the receipt photo.

🧾 FINANCE · receipt DIR-SAMPLE
Sample Guest · GCash · read by the system: PHP 1,500 · confidence high

The booking is confirmed. The calendar is updated, the guest is e-mailed, the confirmation message is sent, and the OPS group gets a card that says CONFIRMED. If someone else already decided, you see Already reviewed. If the dates were taken in the meantime, you see NOT confirmed.
The request is cancelled. The money row is voided and a guest who wrote in Messenger is told. The dates are freed.

Confirm or decline a payment

  1. Open the receipt card in the Finance group.
  2. Read what the system read from the photo: amount, channel, reference. Read any warning such as SHORT.
  3. Open your GCash or bank app. Check the money is really there. The photo alone is not proof.
  4. Tap Confirm booking if the money is there. Tap Decline if it is not.
  5. Read the answer on the card.

Posts money at once Neither button has an Undo. Only staff whose Telegram is linked to a staff login can tap them. If you see your Telegram account is not mapped to a staff profile, the buttons stay for someone else. Ask the owner to link your account, or use the dashboard route below.

The same job in the dashboard

You can decide the same booking in the dashboard instead. Use it when you are at a computer or the Telegram tap is refused.

  1. Open Finance > Review queue. Find the payment under Payment evidence comparisons.
  2. Type a Reason. Tap Approve (or Reject).
  3. Open Bookings > Inquiries. Tap Confirm (or Decline) on the card, then Confirm in the window.
  4. Read the message Confirmed and the guest name.

In the dashboard, Decline does not message the guest. Write to them in Messenger. Tapping Decline on the Telegram card does tell a Messenger guest. Details of every dashboard button are in Inquiries.

A receipt that is late

If a receipt sits for 2 to 3 hours with nobody deciding, one more card appears: ATTENTION · receipt waiting. It says: scroll up to the receipt card and tap Confirm booking or Decline. It is posted once.

Holds: 24 hours to pay

A hold keeps the dates for the guest while you check the payment. If the guest has not paid 24 hours after asking, the system releases the hold by itself. It runs every hour. It frees the calendar dates, voids the money row, and posts this card.

🟡 ATTENTION · hold expired DIR-SAMPLE
Sample Guest · no payment in 24 hours. The dates are free again.
📨 Hello Sample Guest, we have not received your payment yet, so we released the dates. If you still want to stay, tell us and we will check them again.
Posts the message alone in a box. Press and hold it to copy. It copies nothing by itself and sends nothing to the guest.
Asks Cassy to write a warmer or shorter version. She only replies with a draft. Nothing is sent to the guest.

If a guest tells you they paid another way after the hold expired, tell the owner. The owner restores the booking. Do not make a second booking for the same dates.

If a guest cancels

  • Use Cancel booking on the booking page in the dashboard. See Booking detail. The guest gets a message from the system and the dates are freed.
  • No refund is made by that button. The owner or Marifel handles a refund by hand. The rule: 5 or more days before check-in the reservation fee is refunded. Under 5 days it is kept.
  • Airbnb bookings follow Airbnb's own cancellation rules.

Messages the system sends by itself

After you confirm a direct booking, the system sends these messages. All times are Manila time. Each message goes out once per booking. It goes on Messenger if the guest wrote to the page in the last 23 hours, otherwise by e-mail. If it cannot send, the card in the OPS group says so, and you send it yourself.

The door code

Every guest gets a new door code. The system never sends it and never knows it. You set the code in the lock app and send it to the guest yourself. This guide never shows a code. Never write one in a group chat.

🔑 DOOR CODE READY TO SEND · DIR-SAMPLE
Sample Guest · check-in soon · ID on file
Deposit and balance: received (sample)
📨 Hello Sample Guest, your door code for your stay is [door code]. Please keep it private.
Posts the message alone in a box. It has a marked spot where the code goes. Copy the text, type the new code into the marked spot, then send it to the guest yourself. Nothing is sent by the button.
  1. Wait for the DOOR CODE READY TO SEND card.
  2. Check the card says the deposit and balance are received. If it shows a warning, ask the owner before you go on.
  3. Set a new code for this guest in the lock app.
  4. Tap Show as text. Copy the text. Put the new code in the marked spot.
  5. Send it to the guest yourself, on Messenger or by e-mail.

Cleaners do not need this. If a guest cannot get in, see When a guest is handed to the host.

One booking from request to thank-you

  1. Request. The guest submits the form. The dates are held for 24 hours. A NEW BOOKING card arrives.
  2. Receipt. The receipt card arrives. You check the money and tap Confirm booking.
  3. Confirmation. The guest gets message 1. The OPS group sees a CONFIRMED card.
  4. Arrival details. Message 2 goes out between 15:00 and 21:00.
  5. Door code card. Once the ID is on file. You set a new code and send it.
  6. Balance and deposit. The guest pays the balance and the PHP 1,000 deposit. The door code card shows if they were received.
  7. Stay begins. The morning card in OPS lists the arrival.
  8. Refresh. Message 4, for stays of 5 nights or more.
  9. Check-out reminder. Message 5.1.
  10. Thank-you. Message 5.2 with the review links, unless a complaint is open.
