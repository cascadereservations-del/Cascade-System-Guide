Cassy is the team's assistant inside Telegram. You ask her a question and she looks it up. She helps you draft replies. She never sends anything to a guest, and she never changes anything until a person taps a button.

Cassy also answers guests on Messenger. That is a different job. The two share a voice, not a job. This chapter is about the Cassy you talk to in the Telegram groups. For the guest side, see Guests on Messenger.

Ask her in any of these ways:

  1. Start your message with her name: Cassy, who arrives this week? The words cassy or @cassy both work.
  2. Or type /cassy and your question. For a harder question type /deep and your question.
  3. Or tap Ask Cassy (or Cassy) in the menu, then reply to the bot's message with your question.
  4. Or mention the bot, or reply to one of its messages.

Cassy ignores chat that is not for her. You do not need to worry about talking to each other in the group.

  • She answers in the language you used: English, Tagalog, Bisaya or Taglish.
  • Her answers have a fixed shape: one decision line, at most five short lines, one Do: action, then the name of the model she used.
  • She remembers only the last few turns of the chat. A short follow-up like "and next week?" works. A long thread does not. Ask a full question if she seems lost.
  • /deep has a small daily limit. Past the limit she says so and answers on the normal model.

What she can start, and what needs your tap

She can prepare a few things. Each one only posts a card. Nothing is saved until a person taps a button on that card.

  • Log an expense. Finance only. When a Finance message says money was spent with an amount, she raises a confirm card.
  • Save a notice (brownout, holiday, event, reminder). OPS and Finance.
  • Teach a house fact. Finance only. See below.

What she refuses or will not do

  • She never sends anything to a guest. Drafts are for you to copy. Each draft ends with "Nothing was sent."
  • She answers only from what her tools return. If a tool fails she says "I could not read" that thing and stops. She does not ask for access.
  • In OPS she never sees money. She also refuses to log an expense or teach a fact there. She says this can only be done in the Finance group.
  • She never says a card was sent when none was.
  • She cannot void or edit an expense, confirm or cancel a booking, refund a guest, change rates or open the door.
  • She will not save a door code, a payment number or a login as a house fact. If she is unsure who may see a fact, she picks the narrowest level.
  • If the AI service is down she says "I could not reach the model. Try again in a minute."

Walkthrough - draft a reply to a guest

Use this when a guest wrote to the Facebook Page inbox or the Airbnb app and you want a good reply fast.

Pick one of these:

  1. Type /draft and paste what the guest wrote.
  2. Or type cassy reply: and paste the guest's message.
  3. Or tap Guest reply and reply with the pasted text or a screenshot of the chat.
  4. Or send a screenshot with the caption cassy draft.

You can add guest: Sample Guest to name the guest. Then Cassy also looks at their history.

From a screenshot she reads the chat, works out if it is Messenger or Airbnb, finds the guest's name, and takes the guest's newest messages after your last line.

For Messenger she asks the guest assistant to answer with no sending. The draft then uses the real calendar, the rate card and the house voice. If she cannot reach that assistant you still get a draft, marked "the concierge could not be reached, so dates are not checked". For Airbnb she drafts separately. She never adds links, phone numbers, e-mail or payment outside Airbnb.

She checks what kind of message it is. If it is anything but routine, the header shows a line starting with a warning sign. Follow the rule of thumb:

  • Payment claim - check Finance first.
  • Refund - use the 5-day rule and do not promise money.
  • Cancellation or date change - check the calendar.
  • Complaint - think about calling the guest.
  • Safety - call the guest now.
  • Access problem - check who they are first.
  • Policy exception - it is your decision.

The Cassy quick guide in the Staff app

You do not have to remember what to type. In the Cascade Staff app, open More and tap Quick guide. It takes about two minutes to read.

  1. The guide opens as a set of cards. Each card is one job, for example I finished cleaning, I bought something or Something is broken.
  2. Tap a card. It shows the steps, which group to use, and an example of what you type and what Cassy answers.
  3. Tap All guides to go back to the cards.
  4. The last card is How Cassy answers common FAQs. It shows how Cassy replies to a guest on Messenger for each common question, such as check-in time, parking, pets or brownouts. Under each answer a line says Host takes over when she hands the guest to the host.

The FAQ tab is a sketch. Cassy rephrases her answers every time and in the guest's own language. Use it to know what a guest was told, not to copy and paste.

Teach, edit or retire a house fact

A house fact is a how-to that Cassy and the guest assistant both use, like how the aircon works. Only the Finance group can change facts.

  1. Type cassy teach: and the fact. To change one, type cassy edit aircon: and the new text. To switch one off, type cassy retire aircon.
  2. Cassy rewrites it in the house voice: calm, two to five sentences, "we", no exclamation marks. She picks a short topic name, a level and some keywords in English and Tagalog or Bisaya. If the topic name already exists, she edits that fact.
  3. She posts a card. Read it. It shows the new text, the old text and the keywords. A "Voice check" line warns you if the wording breaks house tone. It is a warning. It does not block.
  4. Tap Save it to save, or Cancel to drop it.

The level decides who may be told the fact:

  • public - anyone on Messenger.
  • guest - only a guest whose stay was checked with their check-in date and the first letter of the booking name.
  • staff - Telegram only.
