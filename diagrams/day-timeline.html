1. All day, in the background Every 5 minutes the alert router sends any waiting alerts. Every 15 minutes the job monitor runs. The Airbnb calendar is checked every 15 minutes. Every hour, guest messages go out at :05, held dates and power notices are checked at :20, and the system check runs at :35.
  2. Old logs are cleared Records older than 90 days are removed. Nothing to do.
  3. Rain check starts Every 2 hours until 20:00. If a guest arrives or leaves today and heavy rain is likely, OPS gets a warning.
  4. Meter photo check Yesterday's meter photos are checked against the readings. Look at any photo that is flagged before you pay the cleaner.
  5. Daily system check The system tests itself. You hear about it only if something needs a look.
  6. Daily digest The day card for OPS: arrivals, departures and what needs doing. On a quiet day it stays silent. Mondays start with the weekly OPS summary.
  7. Missed cleaning check An alert if a check-out in the last 14 days has no cleaning report. File the report in the Cleaner app.
  8. Turnover check Yesterday's turnover is checked. Finance sees a card only if there is a real problem. Mondays also bring the weekly Finance summary.
  9. Payment watch A card if a payment is overdue: an Airbnb payout that has not arrived, or a direct booking fee still unpaid.
  10. Check-out reminders A guest who leaves today gets a reminder between 09:00 and 12:00.
  11. Check-out time Guests leave by 12 noon. The cleaner can start the turnover.
  12. Check-in time Guests can arrive from 2 PM. The unit should be ready.
  13. Arrival details and mid-stay messages Between 15:00 and 21:00, guests arriving in 2 days get their arrival details and balance reminder. Long stays get a mid-stay message.
  14. Thank-you message Between 16:00 and 22:00 on check-out day, guests get a thank-you and review links. If a complaint is open, the host writes it by hand instead.
